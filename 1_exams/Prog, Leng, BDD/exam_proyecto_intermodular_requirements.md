Summary of last week's class on Proyecto Intermodular I:

Project presentations will be 10 minutes per student, with a maximum of 5 minutes for questions. Questions will only be asked if the panel has doubts. If during the presentation you make your authorship and above all your learning and control over the project clear, and the scope of the project matches what is expected, there will be few or no questions.

The presentation will follow this structure:
1 minute for student introduction
4 minutes to show the application running — show me the app
4 minutes to show the code and database — show me the code
1 minute for closing and conclusion

There are 30 students. Assuming a worst-case scenario of 15 minutes per student, presentations will take 7.5 hours and will therefore be split into two sessions:
22 May (respecting and not overlapping Teresa's English exam)
29 May

The minimum panel member will be me. The rest of the teaching staff and school management are also invited to attend. Presentations will be closed to the public by default, but you may bring guests if you wish.

Proyecto Intermodular I theoretically continues in second year as Proyecto Intermodular II. Intermodular I counts for 30% of the final cycle grade, Intermodular II for 70%. (Possible regulatory changes may affect this — if so, they would come into effect this summer as is usual with GVA.)

Third trimester grading breakdown:
40% — working project
30% — presentation
30% — document, filled in to the extent we have worked on it in class

Submitting a working project is a mandatory condition to be graded. The presentation is also mandatory.

Tips:
Dress reasonably smartly. Present standing up as much as possible, or seated at the teacher's desk facing the panel, not the screen. Treat the presentation like a mock job interview — present with assertiveness, positivity, and professionalism. The panel is on your side. Real job interviews won't always be. Bring a small bottle of water. Prepare a one-page outline with bullet points. Time yourself at home — going over 10 minutes is penalised.

To select your presentation slot, go to https://exposiciones.jocarsa.com/, create a new user and fill in all the fields with your project details, then request your preferred slots. José Vicente will assign times based on conflicts.
